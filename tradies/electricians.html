A practical electrician call flow

A customer calls while you are on site, driving between jobs or unable to safely answer. Ellie answers in your business context, asks what they need, captures the details your workflow requires and follows the next step you configured — such as booking a supported appointment, taking a message or escalating the call.

Urgent electrical enquiries need business-defined rules

Electrical calls can involve safety concerns. Ellie can ask approved questions and follow urgency or transfer rules that your business defines, but it should not diagnose electrical faults, provide safety-critical electrical advice or replace emergency services. For immediate danger, callers should follow the appropriate emergency guidance.

Keep your existing business number

In many setups, calls from an existing business line can be forwarded to Ellie. The exact phone configuration is confirmed during onboarding, so customers can continue calling the number they already know.

AI receptionist vs voicemail for electricians

Voicemail records information for someone to review later. Ellie is designed to have a live conversation and complete more of the approved front-desk workflow while the caller is still on the line.

Frequently asked questions

Can Ellie answer electrical calls after hours?

Yes. Ellie can answer incoming calls 24/7 and follow the after-hours handling rules configured for your business.

Can Ellie handle emergency electrical calls?

Ellie can identify information and follow urgency or escalation rules that you configure. It should not diagnose faults or provide safety-critical electrical instructions.

Can Ellie book electrical jobs?

With a supported calendar or booking setup, Ellie can check availability and create appointments during the call. Other workflows can capture the enquiry for your team to schedule.

Does Ellie replace my current phone number?

Not necessarily. Call forwarding can often route calls from your existing business number to Ellie. The appropriate setup depends on your current phone service.
