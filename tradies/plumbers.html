Emergency callouts and after-hours enquiries

Plumbing businesses often receive calls outside the moments when office staff are free to answer, including genuine emergencies. Ellie provides an always-available call-handling layer, while your business remains in control of what counts as urgent, what information is collected and which calls should be escalated immediately.

Use your existing business number

In many setups, calls from an existing business line can be forwarded to Ellie. The exact configuration is confirmed during onboarding, so businesses may be able to keep advertising the number customers already know.

Clear boundaries for technical and urgent issues

Ellie is a front-desk service, not a licensed plumber or emergency service. It can collect information and follow urgency or escalation rules that your business defines, but it should not diagnose faults, instruct callers to perform repairs, or invent safety or technical advice.

Frequently asked questions

Can Ellie handle emergency plumbing calls?

Ellie can collect caller-provided details and follow the urgency and escalation rules your business defines, such as flagging a burst pipe or no-hot-water call for immediate callback. It does not diagnose plumbing faults or replace emergency trades advice.

Can Ellie book plumbing jobs into my calendar?

With a supported calendar or booking setup, Ellie can check availability and create appointments during the call. Other workflows can capture the job details for your team to schedule manually.

Will Ellie understand plumbing terminology?

Ellie is briefed on the services, job types and terminology your plumbing business uses, so she can capture accurate details such as the issue type, property access notes and urgency level.

Do I need a new phone number?

Not necessarily. Call forwarding can often route calls from an existing business number to Ellie. The appropriate setup depends on your current phone service.
