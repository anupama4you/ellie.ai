Peak-season and after-hours enquiries

Air conditioning businesses can receive calls outside the moments when office staff are free to answer. Ellie provides an always-available call-handling layer, while your business remains in control of what information is collected and which calls should be escalated.

Use your existing business number

In many setups, calls from an existing business line can be forwarded to Ellie. The exact configuration is confirmed during onboarding, so businesses may be able to keep advertising the number customers already know.

Clear boundaries for technical and urgent issues

Ellie is a front-desk service, not an HVAC technician or emergency service. It can collect information and follow urgency or escalation rules that your business defines, but it should not diagnose faults, instruct callers to perform repairs, or invent safety advice.

Frequently asked questions

Can Ellie answer HVAC calls after hours?

Yes. Ellie can answer incoming calls 24/7 and follow the after-hours handling rules configured for your business.

Can Ellie book air conditioning service appointments?

With a supported calendar or booking setup, Ellie can check availability and create appointments during the call. Other workflows can capture the enquiry for your team to schedule.

Can Ellie handle urgent breakdown calls?

Ellie can collect caller-provided information and follow escalation rules your business defines. It does not diagnose HVAC faults or replace emergency or qualified technical advice.

Do I need a new phone number?

Not necessarily. Call forwarding can often route calls from an existing business number to Ellie. The appropriate setup depends on your current phone service.
