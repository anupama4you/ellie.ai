More than an answering bot

Ellie is configured around your staff, services, opening hours and business rules. When a caller needs something Ellie has not been given, she does not invent an answer. The goal is to handle routine front-desk work reliably and make it clear when your team needs to step in.

What Ellie can handle

  • Answer incoming calls day and night.
  • Understand the reason for the call and capture useful enquiry details.
  • Answer approved questions about your business, services and hours.
  • Check supported calendar availability and book appointments.
  • Send booking confirmations and capture messages for your team.

Built around Australian businesses

Ellie is designed for Australian service businesses that cannot always stop to answer the phone. That includes beauty and wellness businesses, medical and dental clinics, trades and other appointment or enquiry-driven teams.

AI receptionist vs a basic call answering service

A traditional answering service primarily takes the call and passes information on. Ellie is designed to complete more of the workflow during the conversation, such as qualifying the enquiry or booking an appointment when the required integration and business rules are configured.
