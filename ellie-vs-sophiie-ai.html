Where Sophiie AI might be the better fit

Sophiie AI positions itself as a broader "operating system" for trade businesses — its own site references quoting, invoicing and staff scheduling alongside call answering, not just the phone-answering piece. If you want that wider toolset bundled into one platform, it's worth a look. Its $0 entry tier also means you can try the platform itself without any spend, even though that free tier doesn't include calls or SMS.

Where Ellie might be the better fit

Ellie isn't limited to trades — the same configured-setup approach is available for medical and dental clinics and beauty and salon businesses too, if your work spans more than one of those. And because our team configures Ellie around your specific business rather than you working through a self-serve platform, there's less setup work on your side, backed by a 7-day free trial you can start without a card.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sophiie AI cheaper than Ellie?

Sophiie AI publishes a free tier and paid plans starting from $99/month, similar to Ellie's $99/month founding-customer rate. Sophiie's tiers scale with call and SMS volume, while Ellie's published rate is a flat monthly price — compare based on your expected call volume.

Does Sophiie AI work outside the trades industry?

Sophiie AI positions itself specifically for trade and service businesses. Ellie is configured for a wider range of businesses, including medical and dental clinics and beauty and salon businesses, as well as tradies.

Can I switch from Sophiie AI to Ellie?

Yes. Ellie's setup is handled by our team based on your business's services, hours and call-handling rules, so you're not migrating a self-service configuration yourself.

Which one offers a free trial?

Ellie offers a 7-day free trial with no card required. Sophiie AI offers a free tier with limited AI actions and no calls or SMS included, rather than a time-limited trial of its paid plan.
