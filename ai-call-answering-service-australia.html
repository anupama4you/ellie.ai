AI answering service vs voicemail and message-taking

Voicemail records a message after the caller has already reached a dead end. A traditional answering service can take a message for your team. Ellie is designed to handle more of the front-desk workflow during the conversation, while staying within the business rules and integrations you configure.

Who Ellie is built for

Ellie is aimed at enquiry- and appointment-driven Australian businesses where staff cannot always stop what they are doing to answer the phone.

  • Trades and home services — capture job enquiries while the team is on site.
  • Medical and dental clinics — handle routine patient calls and supported appointment workflows.
  • Beauty and wellness — handle service and booking enquiries while staff are with clients.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ellie available after hours?

Yes. Ellie can answer incoming calls 24/7.

Can Ellie use my existing business number?

In many setups, calls from an existing business line can be forwarded to Ellie. The exact phone setup is confirmed during onboarding.

Can callers book over the phone?

Yes, where a supported booking or calendar integration is configured. Ellie can check availability and make the booking during the conversation.

Is this a self-service answering bot?

Ellie is positioned as a managed AI front desk. The setup is configured around your business rather than relying on a generic script.
