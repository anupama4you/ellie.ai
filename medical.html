How It Works

How Ellie handles every patient call.

We set it up around your practice — you just keep doing what you do best. Here's exactly what happens on every call.

Step 1

A patient calls your clinic

Whether reception is mid-appointment, on lunch, or it's 9pm — Ellie picks up every time.

Step 2

Ellie answers in 2 seconds

Warm and professional, sounding like a genuine member of your front desk team — never a script-reader.

Step 3

Books, reschedules, or triages

Step 4

Built For Healthcare Front Desks

Designed around how a clinic actually runs.

Ellie isn't a generic chatbot wearing a stethoscope. She's built specifically to sit alongside a real reception team — handling the calls that would otherwise be missed, without ever stepping into territory that belongs to your clinicians.

Scheduling only — never medical advice

Ellie handles bookings, enquiries, and rescheduling. Any clinical question is acknowledged warmly and passed straight to your team — she never attempts to diagnose or advise.

Urgent calls get flagged, not queued

Ellie is briefed on your practice so she can recognise when a caller needs to be prioritised — and makes sure it reaches your team immediately, day or night.

Books straight into your calendar

Connects to your Google Calendar to check real availability and confirm appointments — no double-bookings, no manual re-entry for your team.

Handled with the same care as your desk

Patient details are only ever used to book and confirm the appointment they called about — nothing more, nothing shared beyond what your own reception team already handles.
