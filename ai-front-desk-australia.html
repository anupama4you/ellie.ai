What does “AI front desk” mean?

An AI receptionist and an AI front desk overlap, but the useful distinction is workflow. Answering a call is only the first step. A front desk also needs to understand why someone called, collect the right information and move the enquiry to an appropriate next action. Ellie is configured around your staff, services, hours and call-handling rules so those actions match the way your business actually operates.

AI front desk vs AI call answering

AI call answering: primarily solves whether an incoming call gets picked up.
AI receptionist: adds conversational handling such as common questions, message taking and routing.
AI front desk: describes the broader configured workflow — answering, qualifying, booking where supported, recording the interaction and escalating when needed.

These are category descriptions rather than rigid industry standards; providers use the terms differently. When comparing services, check the actual workflow each product supports.

Questions to ask before choosing an AI front desk

Can it use your real business information? What happens when it does not know an answer? Can it follow your escalation rules? Does booking work with your actual workflow? Can you review calls afterwards? Can you keep your existing phone number? These operational questions are more useful than choosing a provider based on the phrase “AI receptionist” alone.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI front desk?

An AI front desk handles more of the front-desk workflow than simply picking up the phone. With Ellie, that can include answering calls, qualifying enquiries, answering approved business questions, booking supported appointments, taking messages and escalating according to your rules.

How is it different from an answering service?

A basic answering service may capture a message for later. An AI front desk can continue the conversation and complete configured next steps while the caller is still on the line.

Can Ellie use my existing number?

In many setups, yes: calls from an existing business line can be forwarded to Ellie. Your carrier and preferred call flow determine the appropriate configuration.

Can Ellie transfer a caller to a person?

Ellie can follow configured escalation and transfer rules where the setup supports them. You decide which situations should be transferred, messaged or handled another way.
